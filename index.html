Your groups, rules and prompts belong to your Chrome profile, not to the extension. Updating never overwrites them, and a different Chrome profile starts fresh with its own.

Installing

  1. From the Chrome Web Store — the recommended way. Click Add to Chrome above and Chrome keeps it up to date on its own.
  2. From the .zip — download it, unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
Two things about the .zip. It does not update itself — you repeat those steps for each new version. And Chrome treats it as a separate extension, so it keeps its own groups and rules rather than sharing them with a Web Store copy.

Getting started

  1. Click the Tab Organizer icon, then Open Dashboard.
  2. Under Tab Groups, write the groups you want and a line about what belongs in each. Or add an AI key under Settings and let it suggest a set from the tabs you have open.
  3. Go to Classify Tabs, run it, review what it proposes, and apply. Nothing moves until you say so.

Teach it as you go: when a tab lands in the wrong place, write a rule for that site once and it is decided for free from then on.

What leaves your browser

Nothing, until you use an AI feature. Then the titles and addresses of the tabs being sorted — and your group names and descriptions — go to the provider you chose, with your own key. There is no server of ours, no account and no analytics. Full details on the privacy page.
